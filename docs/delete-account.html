Delete your LexiShikhi account

LexiShikhi users can delete their account and synced learning data directly from the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Open LexiShikhi.
  2. Open Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Google / Email Login.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Complete the re-authentication step and confirm deletion.

What is deleted

The Firebase Authentication account and the LexiShikhi Cloud Firestore learning document associated with the account are deleted. This includes synced coin balance, unlocked topics and practice packs, saved items, practice progress, and related app-state records stored by LexiShikhi.

If you cannot access the app

Use the developer contact email shown on the LexiShikhi Google Play listing and request deletion of your LexiShikhi account. Include the email address used to sign in so the account can be identified. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Third-party transaction records

Google Play, payment providers, or advertising providers may retain records they are independently required to keep under their own policies, security requirements, or applicable law. LexiShikhi does not control those independent retention obligations.
