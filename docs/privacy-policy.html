Privacy Policy for LexiShikhi

Last updated: September 9, 2026

LexiShikhi is a bilingual English-learning app. It provides study content, practice packs, saved items, coin-based unlocks, optional account sync, optional rewarded advertising, and Google Play in-app purchases.

1. Information processed by the app

LexiShikhi may process app preferences and learning data such as language choice, dark-mode preference, coin balance, unlocked topics, unlocked practice packs, saved items, quiz/practice progress, reward history, and purchase-delivery status.

2. Account information and Firebase

If you choose Google or email/password sign-in, Firebase Authentication may process identifiers associated with your account, such as your Firebase user ID, email address, display name, and profile photo when available. LexiShikhi may store your learning progress and coin-related app data in Cloud Firestore so that supported devices can sync the account.

3. Local storage

Some settings and progress information are also stored locally on the device so the app can remain responsive and preserve learning state. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information that has not been synced.

4. Rewarded advertising

LexiShikhi does not intentionally show forced banner, interstitial, or app-open ads. A rewarded ad is requested only after the user chooses the rewarded-ad option. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, consent, fraud-prevention, and ad-performance information according to their own policies. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform may present privacy or consent choices before ads are requested.

5. In-app purchases

Coin packs are processed through Google Play Billing. Product availability and localized prices are provided by Google Play. LexiShikhi does not receive or store full payment-card details. The app receives purchase status and product information needed to deliver purchased coins.

6. How information is used

Information is used to provide account sign-in, synchronize learning progress, maintain coin and unlock state, deliver purchases and rewards, save user preferences, prevent duplicate rewards, and operate the app.

7. Sharing and third-party services

LexiShikhi does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers that are necessary for app features, including Google Firebase, Google Play, and Google AdMob. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

8. Account deletion and data control

Signed-in users can request account deletion from inside the app by opening Settings → Google / Email Login → Delete account. This removes the Firebase Authentication account and the synced LexiShikhi learning document associated with that account, subject to any data that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record obligations of third-party providers.

If you cannot access the app, follow the instructions on the public Account Deletion page and use the developer contact information shown on the LexiShikhi Google Play listing.

9. Children's and teen privacy

LexiShikhi is an educational app. The app does not ask users to publicly post personal information. Account and advertising features are handled through Google services and may be subject to age, family, consent, and regional requirements. The app's Play Console target-audience and advertising configuration should be kept consistent with the audiences to whom the app is distributed.

10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit Firebase data access to the authenticated user's own account. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, providers, or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact

For privacy or account-deletion questions, use the developer contact information displayed on the LexiShikhi Google Play listing.
